## 4. Benefits and Allowances

### 4.2 Remote-Work Equipment Stipend

Employees with an approved remote-work arrangement receive a taxable equipment
stipend. The stipend is paid with the regular payroll.

- The stipend is limited to **200 USD per pay period**, regardless of whether the
  employee is paid weekly, biweekly or monthly.
- The stipend is paid as taxable wages. It is not an accountable-plan
  reimbursement.
- Amounts above the limit are not paid.
